## Pattern summary (what the 1M+ education Reels share)
- **Spectacle first frame.** Every 1M+ example opened on a striking visual, not a face: molten gold pour (8.2M), a sky full of falling elephants (2.6M), 3D-animated story scenes (5.8M, 1.1M). The same creators' talking-head posts sat at ~75K–185K.
- **Bold short text labels + arrows** on the visual (Cleo Abram: neon-yellow labels and giant arrows pointing at the thing).
- **Small, clean bottom captions** — the visual does the shouting, the captions just follow the voice.
- **Story / series framing** — "Part 1 of a 7-part series", narrative 3D shorts, "here's what happened".
- **Our own page (@dynkeducation, 11 posts, ~150–580 plays):** dark story style already; best = "Nobody knows who owns this" (579, mystery hook). Story topics (Mt Gox Feb 2014, 8,000 BTC on a lost drive) are the right instinct — the opening frames are text-heavy and dark, so the move is a brighter, more visual first frame.

### Humphrey Yang — gold refinery tour (Part 1 of 7) — @humphreytalks — 8.2M
- Hook — visual: molten gold pouring into bar moulds, glowing orange, close-up · audio: voiceover straight in (inferred) · text: small bottom caption only
- Format: process B-roll + VO, series ("Part 1 of the gold factory tour")
- **Copy:** open on a satisfying real-world process; series titles; small captions.
- **Don't copy:** factory footage we don't have — our version is a phone/process animation or `inbox/` footage.
- URL: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DArRvdyAh1m/
- Contrast: his talking-head reels on the same grid: 74.7K–185K.

### Cleo Abram — surreal science explainers — @cleoabram — 2.6M (elephants), 858K, 338K, 228K
- Hook — visual: one impossible/huge image fills the frame (dozens of elephants falling from the sky; a glowing sun; a car on a vertical track) · text: neon labels on objects ("IONIC LIQUID"), giant arrows pointing at the subject
- Format: optimistic science/tech explainer, VO over striking visuals, fast cuts
- **Copy:** one huge visual metaphor for an abstract idea (e.g. "1,100,000 BTC nobody can touch" as a mountain of coins in a vault); orange labels + arrows on the subject.

### Zack D. Films — 3D animated "how things work" stories — @zackdfilms — 5.8M, 1.1M, 935K, 805K
- Hook — visual: 3D-rendered characters mid-situation (a crocodile attack, someone taking a chair apart) · text: one short phrase centred low ("no matter what", "finally took it apart")
- Format: 3D narrative explainer, every shot moves, single idea
- **Copy:** narrative "what happens if…" spine; a single short centred phrase as text; constant motion.

### Mark Rober — science entertainment — @markrober — 5.7M, 2.9M, 2.2M
- Hook — visual: face + an unexpected object/situation in the first frame (drone on a graduation stage, ball pit) · text: minimal
- **Copy:** pair the explainer with a stunt or surprise visual in frame 1.
