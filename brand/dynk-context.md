## Education pillars (what @dynkeducation teaches)
1. **Self-custody** — keys, seed phrases, "not your keys, not your coins", custodial vs non-custodial, what happens when an exchange freezes or fails.
2. **Data sovereignty & privacy** — who owns your data, on-device vs cloud AI, what apps can see.
3. **Safe transacting** — scams, phishing, address poisoning, approvals, how to check a transaction.
4. **How money moves** — fees, settlement speed, stablecoins vs volatile tokens (concepts, no price talk), why sending abroad is expensive with banks.
5. **Wallet basics** — what a wallet actually is, public vs private keys, gas, backups.
