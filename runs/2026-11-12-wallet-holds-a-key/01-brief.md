# 01 — Brief
**Topic:** What a crypto wallet actually is — it holds a key, not coins (beginner, semi-technical). Pillar: self-custody.
**Why now:** Cooper asked for a friendlier video for people getting into crypto.
**Real story spine:** James Howells (Newport, Wales) binned a hard drive in 2013 holding the only private key to 8,000 BTC.
The landfill holds ~1.4 million tonnes; High Court struck out his claim 9 Jan 2025; Court of Appeal refused in Mar 2025.
**Paradox:** the bitcoin isn't in the tip — it never left the blockchain. Only the key is buried.
**One idea:** coins live on a public ledger; your wallet is a key; the 12 words are that key in human form.
**Compliance:** bitcoin amounts only (no $/£ values), no price talk, no product claims, no likeness of the person.
