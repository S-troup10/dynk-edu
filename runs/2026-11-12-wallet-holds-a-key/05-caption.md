His 8,000 bitcoin aren't in the rubbish tip. His key is. 🔑

A crypto wallet doesn't hold coins. It holds a key. The coins sit on a public ledger that anyone can read, and only the key can move them. That's what your 12 words are: the key, written so a human can copy it.

Lose the phone → fine, if you have the words.
Lose the words → nobody can help.
Share the words → someone else has your key.

Save this for when you set up your first wallet, and send it to someone who's about to.
