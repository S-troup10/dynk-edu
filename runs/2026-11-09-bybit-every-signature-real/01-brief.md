# Brief — "$1.5B, and every signature was real" (Bybit, Feb 2025)
Pillar: safe transacting / self-custody | Audience: anyone who taps Confirm in a wallet | DYNK shown: none (account brands it).
Takeaway: What you sign matters more than your keys; check your wallet's own screen and don't sign what you can't read.
Spine: real case (largest theft ever) → paradox (no keys stolen; own signers approved) → mechanism (website code showed one tx, signature did another) → turn (you do this every time you tap confirm) → that's why ×3 (fake airdrop "just sign" / permit; check wallet not website; unlimited or unreadable → don't sign) + proof (Scam Sniffer 2024) → twist back → CTA.
Facts (sources in caption):
- 21 Feb 2025: Bybit lost >$1.4B (~$1.5B), 401,347 ETH, from its cold wallet (NCC Group, 10 Mar 2025; FBI PSA I-022625-PSA, 26 Feb 2025, attributed to North Korea, "TraderTraitor").
- Malicious JavaScript was injected into the Safe{Wallet} web UI via a compromised developer machine; the UI showed a routine transfer while signers approved a request (delegatecall) that handed control of the cold-wallet contract to the attackers; raw data not human-readable; signers blind-signed (NCC Group).
- Scam Sniffer 2024 report: ~$494M stolen by wallet drainers / phishing signatures in 2024, 332,000 victim addresses; Permit the top method in large losses.
Must NOT: name/show real people; real logos; claim any wallet makes you scam-proof; price talk.
