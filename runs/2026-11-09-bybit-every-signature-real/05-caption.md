# Caption

The biggest crypto theft ever: $1.5 billion. Nobody stole the keys. The signers approved it themselves. ✍️

Their screens showed a routine transfer. Hidden code in the wallet's website had swapped what they were actually signing, and that signature handed over the whole wallet.

You do the same thing every time you tap Confirm:
• Fake airdrop sites just ask you to "sign". One signature can let them spend your tokens.
• Check what your wallet says, not what the website says.
• If it says "unlimited", or you can't tell what it does, don't sign.
