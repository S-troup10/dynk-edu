# Caption

He lost $91M in one transaction. Nobody cracked his 12 words. Nobody could. 🔐

Your recovery phrase is 12 words from a list of 2,048. That's 2¹²⁸ possible phrases: if all 8 billion people guessed a billion a second, trying them all would take about 100× the age of the universe.

So thieves don't guess. They get you to hand the words over:
• "Support" will never ask for your 12 words. Real support never needs them.
• Screenshots of your phrase can be read by malware.
• Any site that asks you to "verify" your phrase is the thief.

Save this, and send it to someone who keeps theirs in their camera roll.
